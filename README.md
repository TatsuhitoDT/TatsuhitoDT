I work as a **Forward Deployed Engineer** at the intersection of customer operations, business strategy, and applied AI. I go into the field, define the real problem with the people who own it, and stay accountable until the solution is operating in production.

## About Me

I am a business builder operating at the intersection of strategy, capital, operations, and applied AI.

Over two decades, I have led organizations as a **Chief Executive Officer** directing teams of more than **250 employees**, served as an **Executive Officer at GMO Payment Gateway** in fintech, and advised enterprises as a **management consultant** on transformation, M&A, post-merger integration, and ASEAN expansion.

Today, I build and operate AI-native ventures under **Dragon Tech**. I combine executive judgment with hands-on delivery: translating ambiguous workflows into requirements, building the application and automation layer, integrating it with existing systems, and taking it through production rollout.

My long-term thesis is the *one-person unicorn*: AI should not merely reduce manual work. It should enable new operating models in which a small team—or a single operator—can create and run businesses at unprecedented scale.
